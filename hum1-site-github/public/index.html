Why it matters

Your customers are searching right now. Will they find you?

A website is no longer a nice extra. It is the first thing people check before they call, visit or buy. If they cannot find you, they choose someone they can.

Without a website

  • Customers search, find nothing, and call a competitor instead.
  • You look small and uncertain next to businesses that have a site.
  • Prices, services and contacts are scattered across chats and posts.
  • Every order depends on someone picking up the phone.
  • Your audience lives on platforms you do not control.

With a Hum1 website

  • You show up in search and on the map, with a page you control.
  • A sharp site earns trust before anyone speaks to you.
  • Services, prices, portfolio and contacts live in one clear place.
  • Orders and enquiries reach your phone, day and night.
  • Your brand, your audience, your rules.

    Trust at first glance

  2. 02

    Your site presents you, answers questions and takes enquiries 24/7, while you sleep, work or travel.

  3. 03

  4. 04

    When someone searches for what you sell, your site and your map listing work together to bring them in.

    Good to know

    Short answers to the questions we hear most.

    How does payment work?

    You pay in four parts: 15% as a deposit to start, 25% after Stage 1 approval (structure and design concept), 30% after Stage 2 approval (development and content) and 30% after final launch and testing.

    Can I pay with cryptocurrency?

    Yes. We accept crypto for fast, direct and secure international transactions.

    Will the price change along the way?

    No. Timeline and cost are fixed after a short initial discussion, with no hidden fees.

    Can I update the site myself?

    With the Full Web Portal, yes. A secure admin panel lets you change prices, products and content without risking the layout.

    How do we get started?

    With a simple 3-minute survey about your vision and basic requirements. We take care of the rest.

    What happens after launch?

    We transfer all rights to your website and help you with your domain. After that you run the site yourself. If you want us to keep looking after it, you can add the optional Website Care plan.

    What if I want changes?

    Every project has three revision stages. We review and adjust the design and structure with you at each key stage.

    What is Website Care?

    An optional monthly service. We update your content, fix issues, check speed and security, make backups and send you a short monthly report, so you can focus on your business.
